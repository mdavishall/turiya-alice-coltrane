## About the Collection

This collection focuses on images and ephemera from the life and professional music career of Alice Coltrane later known as Swamini Turiyasangitananda or simply Turiya. She was an American jazz musician, composer, bandleader, harpist and pianist and Hindu spiritual leader. She was also the wife of John Coltrane and mother of their children.

This concentrated collection featured here includes photographs taken of Alice (and some featuring John) between the years of 1963 and 1974. It also features recorded content to provide more information about the family and home life of one of jazz music's greatest.
